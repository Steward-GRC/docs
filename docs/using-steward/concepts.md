# Concepts

Start here if you are new. These are the big ideas behind Steward — read them before the
[everyday tasks](./everyday-tasks) page, and the rest of this guide will make more sense.

## What a policy or procedure is

A **policy** is a written rule: it tells people in your organisation how to do something the
right way. A **procedure** is a written set of steps: it walks through how to actually carry a
policy out. Both are first-class documents in Steward — they share the same categories, the same
lifecycle and the same acknowledgement model.

Every policy and procedure lives in a **category** (Steward's admin app calls this a **group** —
a naming carryover from the product this app is built on; they are the same thing). A category is
a topic that holds related documents together, like "Safety" or "Human Resources," and categories
make documents easy to find.

## The document lifecycle

A document moves through clear stages from a first draft to a published document:

- **Draft** — Someone writes the first version. A draft is a work in progress, and most people
  cannot see it yet. An author may start from scratch or from a **template** that sets up the
  sections the document needs.
- **In review** — When the draft is ready, the author submits it. It enters an approval
  **workflow** and goes to the right people to decide.
- **Published** — An approver approves the document through every stage. Now it is official, and
  the people who need it can read it.

## Acknowledgement

To **acknowledge** a document means to confirm that you have read it. When you acknowledge,
Steward records that you have seen it, along with the date — like signing your name to say "Yes,
I read this." Some documents are important enough that your organisation needs proof that people
have read them; acknowledgement gives that proof.

## Who can do what (access model)

Access is controlled per category, so the same person may have different rights in different
parts of the category tree. There are four things you can do with a document, and each can be
allowed or denied for you individually:

| Action | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Read | View the document's content |
| Acknowledge | Confirm you have read it |
| Approve | Approve or reject a draft in a workflow |
| Author | Create and edit drafts |
